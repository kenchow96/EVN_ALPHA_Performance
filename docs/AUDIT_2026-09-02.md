## PWM Decision

25 kHz is the performance default. Both 25 kHz and 40 kHz are ultrasonic. Changing carrier frequency does not change the commanded average bridge voltage or the 1 kHz control bandwidth. At 200 MHz:

- 25 kHz: `WRAP=7999`, 8,000 levels, 0.0125% duty increments.
- 40 kHz: `WRAP=4999`, 5,000 levels, 0.0200% duty increments.
- 40 kHz causes 60% more switching events and associated switching loss.

Higher carrier frequency can reduce winding-current ripple, but no isolated current trace or response measurement in this repository demonstrates that 40 kHz improves control. The decision can be revisited only through an otherwise-identical 25/40 kHz A/B with current ripple, thermal, tracking, and duty-smoothness measurements.
